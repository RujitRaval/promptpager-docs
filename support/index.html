PromptPager Support

For setup questions, connection problems, notification issues, or feedback, email rujit@aarolabs.ai.

Quick checks

  1. Confirm the PromptPager bridge is running on your Mac.
  2. Keep your iPhone and Mac on the same network for local discovery and pairing.
  3. Allow Local Network and Notifications when iOS requests them.
  4. Open the Apple Watch app once after installation so it can register and synchronize.

Connection help

If automatic discovery does not find your Mac, enter the Mac's local IP address and port 47900 in PromptPager Settings. Use the six-digit pairing code printed by the bridge.

Notification help

Notifications require permission on iPhone and Apple Watch. Closed-app delivery also requires an authenticated HTTPS push relay configured by the user. The app continues to support local live sessions without that optional relay.

When contacting support: include your iOS/watchOS versions, PromptPager version, bridge version, and a short description of what you expected and what happened. Never send pairing codes, relay keys, or APNs credentials.
