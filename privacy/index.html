Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

PromptPager does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data with the developer or advertising partners.

How PromptPager handles data

PromptPager connects to a bridge that you run and control. Session names, terminal output, prompts, responses, pairing credentials, and connection settings are used only to provide the app's functionality.

Connection settings and credentials are stored locally on your devices. If you configure an optional push relay, PromptPager sends the information required to deliver and answer notifications—including device push tokens and pending-question content—to that relay. You choose and control the relay and are responsible for its privacy and retention practices. PromptPager does not provide a default developer-operated relay.

PromptPager does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Apple services

When notifications are enabled, Apple Push Notification service processes notification delivery according to Apple's privacy policies.

Your choices

You can stop data exchange by disconnecting the bridge, removing relay settings, disabling notifications, or deleting the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored settings.
