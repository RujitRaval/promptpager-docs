Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

PromptPager does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. It processes only the data needed to connect your devices and deliver the product's features.

How PromptPager handles data

PromptPager for Mac runs a private service inside the Mac app. Session names, terminal output, prompts, responses, pairing credentials, and connection settings are used only to provide the app's functionality. Local connections stay between your devices and Mac.

Connection settings and credentials are stored locally on your devices. When remote notifications are enabled, PromptPager uses a developer-operated relay to deliver and answer notifications when a direct local connection is unavailable. The relay receives registered device push tokens, a scoped Mac identity, and pending-question content required for delivery. Device registrations remain until they are unregistered; pending questions and answers expire after no more than one hour. The relay does not receive or store your Claude or Codex credentials.

PromptPager does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Apple services

When notifications are enabled, Apple Push Notification service processes notification delivery according to Apple's privacy policies.

Your choices

You can stop data exchange by disconnecting your Mac, disabling notifications, quitting PromptPager on the Mac, or deleting the apps. Deleting an app removes its locally stored settings. Contact rujit@aarolabs.ai to request deletion of a relay device registration.
